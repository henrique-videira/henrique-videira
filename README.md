### Henrique Videira

I'm a central bank technologist and economist. I work on tokenised settlement, payment-versus-payment across ledgers, clearing mechanism design and AI for supervision. My background is a PhD in Finance and postdoctoral research in Electrical Engineering, on distributed systems, privacy and threat modelling.

**Focus**
- Settlement and interoperability for regulated blockchains
- Joint clearing and liquidity optimisation, where the price and the fee are discovered inside the clearing itself
- Agentic AI for supervision, with human-in-the-loop controls and audit trails
